ANCHOR Studio for macOS
by A. Mamdouh Kastour · A. Mamdouh Kastour’s Scholarly Anchor

1. Drag "ANCHOR Studio" onto "Applications".
2. Open it from Applications. The first time, macOS says it cannot verify the
   developer, because the app is free and not registered with Apple:
   - macOS 15 or newer: click "Done", then open System Settings > Privacy & Security,
     scroll down and click "Open Anyway" next to ANCHOR Studio.
   - macOS 14 or older: right-click the app, choose "Open", then "Open".
3. If R is not on your Mac yet (or a different R version is), the app offers to
   install the R version it includes. This is needed once, and asks for your Mac password.

The app opens in its own window: Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge app mode if
installed, otherwise Safari. It stops by itself about 20 seconds after you close
that window. Your work is autosaved every minute.
If something goes wrong, the log is in ~/Library/Logs/ANCHOR Studio.log
